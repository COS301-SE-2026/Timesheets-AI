Frequently asked questions.

Find quick answers to common questions about using Momently.

How do i track my time?
Select a project and task, then start the timer. When you finish working, stop the timer and your time will be saved to your entries. You can also add a manual time entry if you forget to start a timer while working.
How do i submit my timesheet?
Review your logged timer on the log time page and submit the timesheet from the log time page once you are ready forthe manager's approval.
Why can't i edit my timesheet?
A timesheet may not be editable once it has been submitted or approved. If it was rejected, review the feedback, make the required change and resubmit the timesheet.
What are AI insights?
AI insights analyze your time-tracking data to identify patterns and provide useful information about productivity and project activity.
Why does my project forecast change?
Project forecast can change as new time entries, project changes and workload information become available.
